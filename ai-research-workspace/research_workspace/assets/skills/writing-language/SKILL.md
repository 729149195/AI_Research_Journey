# writing-language

## Inputs

Confirmed question/core argument, section roles, bounded Claims, verified evidence with original locators, relevant counterevidence, target journal/article type, terminology/style rules and current manuscript. Obtain a writing-language task packet; do not treat decisions, prior model answers or search snippets as supporting evidence.

## Workflow

1. Plan the section's contribution to the whole argument. For each paragraph record purpose, lead assertion, supporting evidence and logical transition. Identify missing evidence before composing fluent prose.
2. Draft only what the available records support. Preserve populations, conditions, comparisons, uncertainty and limitations. Separate observations from interpretations and hypotheses. Insert traceable citation keys such as `[@SRC-001]`; never invent bibliographic metadata.
3. Check the entire argument and section-to-section dependencies first. Then check paragraph logic, factual precision and appropriate academic expression. Finally improve readability, natural English, sentence variation, terminology and consistent voice.
4. Replace vague praise, stock transitions, repetitive claims and unsupported intensifiers with concrete research content. Prefer direct explanation without changing inference strength. Keep necessary technical terms and uncertainty; do not remove qualifications merely to sound confident.
5. Cross-check abstract, main results, discussion and conclusion for numerical, terminological and inferential consistency. Check that figure references and captions actually correspond to tracked figure/Claim records.
6. Show substantive edits separately from copyediting. A change to a Claim's meaning, scope, number or strength goes back to logic-methodology and knowledge-evidence; the writer does not independently upgrade scientific assertions.
7. Return full section-node or Markdown write proposals with expected hashes. Preserve stable section markers. Run sync and review after approved changes; never write directly over a conflicting manuscript version.

## Outputs

Reviewable manuscript/section operations, explanation of substantive changes, citation mapping and unresolved evidence/logic questions. Use the supplied base_fingerprint. Unknown facts remain explicitly unresolved rather than filled with plausible prose.

## Boundaries

Fluent language does not establish evidence or novelty. Do not create citations from memory, claim a journal language standard has been met without actual requirements, or promise any AI-detector outcome. Do not self-approve evidence, clear methodological blockers, or impersonate an independent reviewer.

## Evaluation

An unsupported superlative should be removed or returned as an evidence task. A causal-to-association change must propagate beyond its sentence. A beautifully worded but evidence-free paragraph must remain blocked. A pure language edit must preserve technical meaning and uncertainty.
